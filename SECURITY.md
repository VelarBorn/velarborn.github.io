# Reporting something privately

VÉLARBÖRN has no accounts and no servers that hold your data. If you need to tell us something that should not be public, such as a security problem, a privacy request or a copyright notice, please email us:

**velarbornapp@gmail.com**

Or use the form on our support page: https://velarborn.github.io/support.html#message

Please put the topic in the subject, for example "Security", "Privacy request" or "Copyright notice", so we can route it quickly.

For ordinary bugs, questions and feedback you can also open a public issue. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.
