Accessibility Statement

VÉLARBÖRN · Prepared 5 October 2026

In short: We aim for WCAG 2.1 Level AA on this website, the game has accessibility settings, and you can switch all website animation off. Tell us if something is hard to use.

1. Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to read about VÉLARBÖRN and to play it. We are working to make this website, and the game, usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

2. The standard we aim for

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We have checked it ourselves and believe it meets most of those criteria, but it has not been independently audited, so we do not claim full conformance.

3. What we have done on this website

  • A "Skip to content" link, and a clear page structure with headings and landmarks.
  • Full keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator, including the menu and the FAQ.
  • Text and controls with strong colour contrast on a dark background.
  • Descriptive text for meaningful images, and empty descriptions for purely decorative ones.
  • A layout that adapts to any screen size and still works when you zoom or enlarge text.
  • Respect for your device's "reduce motion" setting, plus an "Animations" switch in the footer that turns all movement off and remembers your choice.
  • Content that stays readable if scripts do not run.
  • No flashing content, and no audio or video that plays on its own.
  • Plain, descriptive link and button text.

4. Accessibility in the game

VÉLARBÖRN includes settings that help many players, including:

  • color-blind modes,
  • an adjustable interface size,
  • a reduced motion option and a screen shake switch,
  • haptics you can turn on or off,
  • story text and voice settings,
  • graphics quality and battery saver options,
  • portrait play, designed for use with one hand.

The game is a real-time strategy game drawn with a game engine, so some parts of it may not work well with screen readers such as VoiceOver. We know this is a limitation and we welcome feedback on what would help you most.

5. Known limitations

  • Some decorative artwork has short descriptions rather than long ones. The information in the world map is also given in the text beside it.
  • The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If they fail to load, the page falls back to your device's fonts.
  • We do not control third-party sites we link to.

6. Tell us what is not working

If you find a barrier on this website or in the game, or need information in a different format, please let us know. Email us at velarbornapp@gmail.com, use the form on our support page, or open a public GitHub issue. Tell us what you were trying to do, the page or screen, and the assistive technology you use. We will review every report and do our best to fix the problem.

7. About this statement

This statement was prepared on 5 October 2026 and is based on our own review. We will update it when we make significant changes.
