Raise your hall

From a single hut to a stronghold

Start small and grow with care. Every building does a job, and every job keeps your clan alive.

  • Gather timber from the forest, ore from the mine and fish from the shore.
  • Build barracks, a smithy, towers and more, with extra builders speeding up the work.
  • Upgrade your hall with relics you carry home from the wild.

The journey

Six places, one long road

Take your clan from a quiet island to the gates of a great city, with short side missions along the way.

  1. Hearthfall

    Your home island, where every clan begins.

  2. Skerrymoot Optional

    A strange isle across the water, full of surprises.

  3. Farlanding

    A new shore and a new camp, where the first machines wait.

  4. Gatewatch

    Thor's Wall, and the gate that must be opened.

  5. The Queen's Mouth

    Spore fields below the heart of the hive.

  6. Miklagard

    The Great City, taken district by district.

FAQ

Good questions, noble guest

What is VÉLARBÖRN?

A Norse-fantasy real-time strategy campaign. You gather resources, raise a hall, command your clan and fight back a hostile alien world.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, running iOS 15 or later. It is designed to be played in portrait.

When can I play?

The game is in development and coming to the App Store. Check back here for launch news.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be announced closer to launch.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Apple Game Center is optional and only used to sync your saves through iCloud.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no ads and no analytics. Read the full details in our privacy policy.

I found a bug. Who do I tell?

Head to the support page and tell us what happened.
