VÉLARBÖRN

Raise your banner. Rule the realm.

VÉLARBÖRN is a real-time medieval strategy game of conquest, economy and tactics. Build your base, gather resources and raise an army where every unit is an individual warrior.

Features

  • Real-time strategy: command your units and claim the map.
  • Fog of war: scout the unknown and strike where your rival isn't looking.
  • Ranged and melee combat: arrows, fireballs and siege weapons.
  • Economy and building: gather resources and construct your keep.
