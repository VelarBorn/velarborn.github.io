Privacy Policy

VÉLARBÖRN · Effective 5 October 2026

In short: VÉLARBÖRN has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple Game Center, your saves can sync through your own iCloud account. We do not run servers that collect your personal information, and we never sell it.

1. Who we are

VelarBorn ("we", "us") makes the game VÉLARBÖRN ("the game") and runs this website. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game or visit the site. You can reach us using the details in section 14.

2. What the game stores on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Campaign progress, such as completed stages, skill points, relics and saved bases.
  • Your settings, such as volume, graphics, accessibility and display options.
  • Simple play statistics, such as matches played and won.

This information is not sent to us.

3. Optional Apple sign-in and sync

If you choose to sign in with Apple Game Center, the game receives your Game Center player identifier and display name from Apple so it can tie your saves to your account. Your saves can then sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage.

This is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or see this data. Apple's own privacy policy applies to it: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. What we do not collect

The game does not collect or use:

  • advertising identifiers or any advertising SDKs,
  • analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting of our own,
  • your location, contacts, photos or microphone,
  • payment details or financial information.

If you have switched on "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. These cannot identify you.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Third-party services

The following third parties are involved, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple (Game Center, iCloud, the App Store) if you choose to use those features.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and our public issue tracker.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the fonts used on this website.
  • Google (Gmail), which hosts our support mailbox if you email us.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you in the game, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over any personal information we do hold, including to:

  • know what we hold and ask for a copy,
  • correct or delete it,
  • restrict or object to how it is used, and ask for it to be moved,
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it,
  • complain to your data protection authority.

If you email us, your message is handled in our support mailbox, hosted by Google (Gmail). If you contact us through the public issue tracker instead, what you post is public and processed by GitHub. Where data protection law such as the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our basis for handling a message you send us is our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the game.

California and other US states

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not collect sensitive personal information. California residents and residents of other US states with privacy laws may ask us what we hold, to correct or delete it, and will not be treated differently for doing so.

Do Not Track

We do not track you across sites or services, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to opt you out of.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under the age required by local law). The game contains fantasy violence, so please check its age rating in the App Store. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device, protected by the security features of iOS. Synced saves are protected by Apple's iCloud. We run no servers that hold your game data, which keeps the amount of information at risk very small.

10. International transfers

Apple, GitHub and Google operate globally, so information handled by them may be processed in countries other than your own, under their own safeguards and policies.

11. Keeping and deleting data

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. To remove synced saves from iCloud, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find VÉLARBÖRN and choose Delete Data. Game Center data is managed by Apple.

12. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Your choice to turn animations on or off is remembered in your browser's local storage on your device only, and is never sent to us.

Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details in order to deliver them; see the Google Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the effective date above. If a change is significant we will make it clear on this page.

14. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email us at velarbornapp@gmail.com or use the form on our support page. When you email us we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, and we use it only to reply to you. We keep a message only as long as needed to deal with it, and delete it if you ask.
