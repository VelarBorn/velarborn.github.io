Privacy Policy

VÉLARBÖRN · Effective 4 October 2026

In short: VÉLARBÖRN has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple Game Center, your saves can sync through your own iCloud account. We do not run servers that collect your personal information, and we never sell it.

1. Who we are

VelarBorn ("we", "us") makes the game VÉLARBÖRN and runs this website. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game or visit the site.

2. What the game stores on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Campaign progress, such as completed stages, skill points, relics and saved bases.
  • Your settings, such as volume, graphics, accessibility and display options.
  • Simple play statistics, such as matches played and won.

This information is not sent to us.

3. Optional Apple sign-in and sync

If you choose to sign in with Apple Game Center, the game receives your Game Center player identifier and display name from Apple so it can tie your saves to your account. Your saves can then sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage.

This is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or see this data. Apple's own privacy policy applies to it: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. What we do not collect

The game does not collect or use:

  • advertising identifiers or any advertising SDKs,
  • analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting of our own,
  • your location, contacts, photos or microphone,
  • payment details or financial information.

If you have switched on "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. These cannot identify you.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or tracking.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. To remove synced saves from iCloud, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find VÉLARBÖRN and choose Delete Data. Game Center data is managed by Apple.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond promptly.

9. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details in order to deliver them. See the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on our GitHub issue tracker, and please do not include personal information in a public issue.
