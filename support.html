Common questions

My progress is not syncing between devices

Cloud sync is optional. Make sure you are signed in to Apple Game Center on both devices, that iCloud is turned on for the game in iPhone Settings, and that both devices are online. Progress is otherwise stored only on the device you play on.

I lost my progress

Saved games are kept on your device. Deleting the app removes them unless you had cloud sync switched on. If you reinstalled and sign in to Game Center with iCloud enabled, your saves should come back.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue and tell us your device model, your iOS version, the game version shown in the game, what you were doing, and what went wrong. Screenshots help a lot.

Where are the accessibility options?

Open the game's settings. You can switch on color-blind modes, change the interface size, reduce motion, and turn haptics on or off. Read more on our accessibility page.

The game runs hot or drains my battery

In the game's settings, turn on battery saver and lower the graphics quality. Closing other apps can also help.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To remove synced saves, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, find VÉLARBÖRN and choose Delete Data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
